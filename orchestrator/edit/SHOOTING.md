# Shooting for the editor: what makes a reel look professional before any edit

Zero1 and Aevy look professional mainly because of how they are shot: a lit set, a soft background,
clean sound and a fluent first line. The editor can cut, grade, punch in and add graphics. It cannot fix
dark footage, room echo or a hook said three times. This page is the checklist.

## Phone settings (once)
- **Back camera**, held vertically, on a tripod or stand at **eye level**. The front camera is softer.
- **1080p or 4K at 30 fps.** Don't use 60 fps or "cinematic" mode.
- **Turn HDR video off**. On iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → HDR Video. The editor can tone-map
  HDR footage, but SDR keeps the colours you saw.
- **Lock focus and exposure** on your face (long-press on iPhone, "AE/AF lock"), so brightness doesn't change
  when you move your hands.
- Clean the lens.

## Framing
- **Head and shoulders**, with your eyes about one third down from the top. Leave the **top 15%** clear,
  because the title card sits there for the hook.
- **Keep space under your chin.** Captions go at about 58% of the height and cards in the lower third.
  Don't hold products there.
- **Sit 1–2 m in front of the background**, not against a wall: the gap gives depth. A shelf, plant or
  warm lamp behind you, as on both reference sets, does more than any filter.

## Light
- **One soft light in front of you, about 45° to one side**: a window you face, or a softbox or LED panel
  through a diffuser. Avoid an overhead tube light, which casts shadows under the eyes.
- **Keep the background a little darker than your face.** A warm lamp in the background matches the
  styles' warm grade.

## Sound (this matters most)
- **Use the phone mic 30–50 cm from your mouth, or a lavalier mic** (wired, or a wireless clip-on).
  **Don't use earphones**: Bluetooth mics record only up to about 6 kHz and sound boxy.
- **Turn off the fan and AC while recording.** Your last test recording had a low hum about 30 dB under
  your voice: clean-up softens it, silence removes it.
- **Record in a room with soft things** (curtains, bed, sofa). Bare walls echo, and echo can't be removed
  afterwards.

## Delivery
- **Have the script or 3–5 bullet points in front of you.** Your recording without notes stretched words
  for 2 s while thinking. The editor can cut pauses, but not a stretched "and…".
- **Say the hook first, in one breath.** If you fumble, pause for a second and say the whole sentence
  again. The editor keeps the later take and cuts the earlier one, so there's no need to stop recording.
- **Finish on the takeaway**, then the call to action if you want one.

## Sending it
- **Send the original file**, not a WhatsApp copy (WhatsApp compresses it). Upload it to Drive, share it as
  "anyone with the link", then send `/edit <link> [style] [notes]`.
- **Put in the notes** anything the graphics should show: your name and credential for the lower third,
  numbers you want on screen, where you want to end. Graphics only show numbers you said or wrote in the
  notes.
- **Screenshots or pictures to show**: ask for the job's `assets/` folder and name them in the notes.

## Screen demos
- **Record at 1080p or more**, with a large cursor and notifications off.
- Automatic zooms that follow the cursor need the cursor recorded alongside the video. Recorders like
  OpenScreen do this. The editor can't do it yet; until then, zooms in demos are planned by hand.
