morning-brief

morning-brief is a personal tool that prepares one short summary each morning for its owner. It reads the owner's own mail, chat messages and calendars, read-only, and sends the owner a ranked list of what needs attention.

Who it is for

Only the owner and their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads the subject, sender and short preview of new Gmail messages (read-only access).
  • Reads free/busy times and, when the owner approves, creates meetings in the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Never sends, deletes or changes email.

Details: Privacy policy.

Part of the open-source hermes-agent fork.
